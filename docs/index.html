Welcome to the WES house tour!

This is a self-guided tour. You can choose from a number of different routes, or you can wonder aimlessly.

Choose a route and follow the map to each tour stop. The tour has eight (8) tour stops, so plan about 30 minutes for the tour.

All tours start in the first-floor front hallway.

  • Epsilon Route (Outside first, then kitchen, basement, and finally third floor.)
  • Sigma Route (Start-in-basement and work your way up. Basement, kitchen, exterior, third floor.)
  • 100 Route (Start-on-third-floor and work your way down. Third floor, kitchen, exterior, basement.)
